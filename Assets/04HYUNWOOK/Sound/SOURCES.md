- Title: racing car engine sound loops
- Creator: domasx2
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/racing-car-engine-sound-loops
- Original download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/loop_0.wav
- License: CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Retrieved: 2026-09-07
- The author states these loops were remade from a public-domain recording.

`engine_loop_original.wav` is the unchanged download.
`Engine_Loop.wav` is a normalized, mono PCM version used in all three scenes.
`Engine_Preview.wav` is an eight-second audition of that loop with increasing pitch and volume.
The engine runs quietly at idle; CarEngineAudio increases pitch and volume with actual vehicle speed.

## Active cyber sound replacements

Cyber_Gear_Shift.wav (0.22 s), Cyber_HUD_PowerOn.wav (1.25 s), and Cyber_Helmet_Shield.wav (0.65 s) are procedurally synthesized for this project by Tools/build_cyber_audio.py using FM chirps, sine tones, arpeggios and noise. No external audio samples are used in these replacements. Test 1/2/3 now reference these WAV clips. The original Kenney OGG clips and their license files remain available as alternatives.

### Quick recorded gear variant

Car_Gear_Select_Quick.wav is the active shift clip in Test 1/2/3. Derived from the CC0 Car_Gear_Select.mp3 credited above: kept 0.29-0.67s and 0.95-1.26s, joined with an 8ms crossfade, 2ms attack / 15ms release, peak normalized to 0.8. Duration 0.682 seconds, mono 48kHz PCM. Removes the opening wait and middle pause without changing pitch. Original MP3 retained. HUD_PowerOn_TV.mp3 is unchanged.
